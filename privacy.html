Privacy policy

Your music
never leaves
your phone.

Run2Beat is designed around a simple promise: the music files you provide and the data the app derives from them stay on your device.

Last updated: April 2026

Run2Beat (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use Run2Beat (“the App”).

1. Information We Collect

Run2Beat does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data — including imported music files, BPM lists, playlists, and settings — is stored locally on your device.

2. Music Files

Music files you import into Run2Beat are stored in the App’s private sandbox on your device. They are not shared with third parties, uploaded to any server, or accessed outside the App.

3. Apple Watch Sync

When you sync BPM lists to your Apple Watch, audio files are transferred directly from your iPhone to your paired Apple Watch using Apple’s WatchConnectivity framework. No data passes through our servers.

4. Analytics and Tracking

Run2Beat does not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks. We do not track your usage or collect crash reports beyond what Apple may collect through its standard iOS crash reporting.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party services, social networks, or advertising platforms.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version is always available within the App.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@run2beat.com.
